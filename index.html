PRODUCT CONCEPT · STUDY AI

Study Space

A contextual study environment built around the student's existing study material.

Feature Set • AI + Knowledge Base • Device-accessible context
01 · FEATURE SET

The Subject Knowledge Base is the foundation.

The source document positions the other AI capabilities as layers operating on top of the accumulated study context.

01

Study Material Auto-Organization

Study Space scans device-accessible study content, understands the subject/topic from the content, and organizes it into the relevant subject context.

02

Subject Knowledge Base

Each subject folder acts as a contextual knowledge base, allowing accumulated study material to be used collectively when answering questions or preparing for exams.

03

AI Exam Preparation

The concept uses the student's own study material for preparation, including the possibility of providing 2–3 years of question papers to identify important questions or prepare topic-wise.

02 · EXPERIENCE FLOW

From scattered content to a study context.

The visual in the source document presents three connected stages: collect & organize, understand & prepare, and manage study life.

01 Collect & Organize

Bring device-accessible study content into an organized subject context.

02 Understand & Prepare

Use the accumulated subject context for AI-assisted study and preparation.

03 Manage Study Life

The source visual also shows scheduling, reminders and notification management around the study workflow.

03 · KEY TECHNICAL DISTINCTION

Context should already be available to the AI.

The key distinction described in the source is that Study Space is intended to use device-accessible study context with user permission.

This is positioned against a workflow where the student repeatedly has to collect files and upload them into a separate AI application.

04 · CORE IDEA
“The Subject Knowledge Base is the foundation.”

The concept therefore starts with building persistent subject context, with the other AI capabilities operating on top of that accumulated context.
